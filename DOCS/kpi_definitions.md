# KPI Definitions & Business Metrics

## 📊 Financial & Sales Analytics Dashboard

This document defines all Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and business metrics used in the dashboard.

---

## 📈 1. REVENUE METRICS

### Total Revenue
- **Definition:** Sum of all sales revenue from transactions
- **Formula:** `SUM(revenue)`
- **Business Use:** Primary measure of top-line sales performance
- **Example:** $2,450,123.50
- **Trend:** Track daily, monthly, yearly
- **Target:** Year-over-year growth of 15%+

### Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition:** Average revenue per transaction
- **Formula:** `Total Revenue / Total Transactions`
- **Business Use:** Measures customer spending behavior per order
- **Example:** $185.42
- **Benchmark:** Industry average: $100-$300 (varies by retail type)
- **Improvement Strategy:** Upselling, bundling, minimum order incentives

### Revenue per Customer
- **Definition:** Average revenue generated per unique customer
- **Formula:** `Total Revenue / Unique Customers`
- **Business Use:** Customer value assessment
- **Example:** $1,225.50
- **Action:** Focus on high-value customers for retention

---

## 💰 2. PROFITABILITY METRICS

### Total Profit
- **Definition:** Total profit after cost of goods sold
- **Formula:** `SUM(profit)` or `SUM(revenue - cost)`
- **Business Use:** Bottom-line profitability measure
- **Example:** $845,230.00

### Profit Margin %
- **Definition:** Percentage of revenue retained as profit
- **Formula:** `(Total Profit / Total Revenue) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Efficiency and pricing strategy effectiveness
- **Example:** 34.5%
- **Benchmark:**
  - Electronics: 10-20%
  - Clothing: 40-50%
  - Books: 30-40%
- **Alert:** Flag if below 20% overall

### Gross Margin
- **Definition:** Revenue minus cost of goods sold
- **Formula:** Same as profit in this model
- **Note:** In more complex models, includes additional costs

---

## 📊 3. TIME-BASED GROWTH METRICS

### Year-over-Year (YoY) Revenue Growth
- **Definition:** Revenue change compared to same period last year
- **Formula:** `((Current Year Revenue - Previous Year Revenue) / Previous Year Revenue) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Annual growth tracking
- **Example:** +18.5%
- **Target:** 10-20% YoY growth
- **Interpretation:**
  - Positive: Business is growing
  - Negative: Declining performance, investigate causes

### Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth
- **Definition:** Revenue change compared to previous month
- **Formula:** `((Current Month - Previous Month) / Previous Month) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Short-term trend monitoring
- **Example:** +5.2%
- **Note:** More volatile than YoY, affected by seasonality

### Year-to-Date (YTD) Revenue
- **Definition:** Total revenue from start of year to current date
- **Formula:** `SUM(revenue WHERE date >= Jan 1 of current year)`
- **Business Use:** Progress toward annual goals
- **Example:** $8,250,000 (as of Aug 31)

### Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Revenue
- **Definition:** Revenue from start of current quarter
- **Business Use:** Quarterly performance tracking
- **Target Setting:** Often tied to quarterly business objectives

### Same Period Last Year (SPLY)
- **Definition:** Revenue from equivalent period in previous year
- **Formula:** Revenue from [Date - 1 year]
- **Business Use:** Normalized YoY comparison accounting for seasonality

## 👥 4. CUSTOMER METRICS

### Unique Customers
- **Definition:** Count of distinct customers who made purchases
- **Formula:** `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)`
- **Business Use:** Customer base size tracking
- **Example:** 1,523 customers

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated by a customer over their lifetime
- **Formula:** `SUM(revenue) per customer_id`
- **Business Use:** Identify high-value customers
- **Example:** $3,450 per customer
- **Segmentation:**
  - High-value: CLV > $5,000
  - Medium-value: CLV $1,000-$5,000
  - Low-value: CLV < $1,000

### Average Customer Lifetime Value
- **Definition:** Average CLV across all customers
- **Formula:** `AVG(Customer Lifetime Value)`
- **Business Use:** Customer value benchmarking
- **Example:** $1,225

### Repeat Customer Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of customers who made 2+ purchases
- **Formula:** `(Customers with >1 purchase / Total Customers) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Customer loyalty indicator
- **Example:** 42%
- **Benchmark:**
  - Good: 40%+
  - Excellent: 60%+
- **Strategy:** Loyalty programs, retention campaigns

### Customer Retention Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of customers who return in subsequent period
- **Formula:** `(Customers who purchased in both periods / Customers in first period) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Churn prevention tracking
- **Example:** 67%
- **Improvement:** Email marketing, loyalty rewards

### Average Purchase Frequency
- **Definition:** Average number of purchases per customer
- **Formula:** `Total Transactions / Unique Customers`
- **Business Use:** Customer engagement level
- **Example:** 4.2 purchases per customer
- **Action:** Increase frequency through remarketing

## 🎯 5. RFM SEGMENTATION METRICS

### Recency (Days)
- **Definition:** Days since customer's last purchase
- **Formula:** `MAX(transaction_date) - Current Date per customer`
- **Business Use:** Identify at-risk customers
- **Scoring (1-5):**
  - 5: Last purchase 0-30 days ago (most recent)
  - 4: 31-60 days
  - 3: 61-90 days
  - 2: 91-180 days
  - 1: 180+ days (at risk)

### Frequency (Purchase Count)
- **Definition:** Number of purchases made by customer
- **Formula:** `COUNT(transactions) per customer`
- **Business Use:** Engagement and loyalty measure
- **Scoring (1-5):**
  - 5: 20+ purchases
  - 4: 10-19 purchases
  - 3: 5-9 purchases
  - 2: 2-4 purchases
  - 1: 1 purchase

### Monetary (Total Spend)
- **Definition:** Total amount customer has spent
- **Formula:** `SUM(revenue) per customer`
- **Business Use:** Customer value measurement
- **Scoring (1-5):**
  - 5: $5,000+ spent
  - 4: $2,000-$4,999
  - 3: $1,000-$1,999
  - 2: $500-$999
  - 1: <$500

### RFM Segments
- **Champions:** R=5, F=5, M=5 (Best customers)
- **Loyal Customers:** R≥3, F≥3, M≥3 (Regular buyers)
- **Big Spenders:** M≥4 (High monetary value)
- **Recent Customers:** R≥4, F≤2 (New or returning)
- **At Risk:** R≤2, F≥3, M≥3 (Haven't purchased recently)
- **Lost Customers:** R≤2, F≤2 (Inactive)
- **Regular Customers:** Others

**Marketing Actions:**
- Champions → VIP treatment, early access
- Loyal → Reward programs
- At Risk → Win-back campaigns
- Lost → Reactivation offers

## 📦 6. PRODUCT & CATEGORY METRICS

### Total Products Sold (Quantity)
- **Definition:** Total units sold across all transactions
- **Formula:** `SUM(quantity)`
- **Example:** 125,450 units

### Units per Transaction
- **Definition:** Average quantity per order
- **Formula:** `Total Quantity / Total Transactions`
- **Example:** 2.3 units per order
- **Strategy:** Encourage multi-item purchases

### Category Mix %
- **Definition:** Percentage of revenue from each category
- **Formula:** `(Category Revenue / Total Revenue) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Product mix analysis
- **Example:**
  - Electronics: 45%
  - Clothing: 30%
  - Home & Garden: 15%
  - Sports: 7%
  - Books: 3%

### Product Contribution %
- **Definition:** Individual product's share of total revenue
- **Formula:** `(Product Revenue / Total Revenue) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Identify star products
- **Action:** Focus inventory on top 20% of products

### Top Product Revenue
- **Definition:** Revenue from best-selling product
- **Business Use:** Product performance tracking

---

## 🏪 7. STORE & REGIONAL METRICS

### Store Performance Rank
- **Definition:** Ranking of stores by revenue
- **Formula:** `RANK() by Total Revenue`
- **Business Use:** Store comparison and best practices sharing

### Regional Revenue Share %
- **Definition:** Percentage of revenue from each region
- **Formula:** `(Regional Revenue / Total Revenue) × 100`
- **Example:**
  - West: 35%
  - Northeast: 28%
  - South: 24%
  - Midwest: 13%

### Store vs Average Performance
- **Definition:** How store performs vs company average
- **Formula:** `((Store Revenue - Avg Store Revenue) / Avg Store Revenue) × 100`
- **Example:** +15% above average
- **Action:** Share best practices from top performers

---

## ⏰ 8. TIME-BASED METRICS

### Weekend vs Weekday Revenue
- **Definition:** Revenue split between weekends and weekdays
- **Formula:** `SUM(revenue WHERE is_weekend=1) / Total Revenue × 100`
- **Business Use:** Staffing and promotion planning
- **Example:** Weekend: 35%, Weekday: 65%

### Seasonal Revenue Patterns
- **Definition:** Revenue distribution across seasons
- **Categories:** Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall
- **Business Use:** Inventory and campaign planning
- **Example:** Peak season (Holiday - Nov/Dec): 30% of annual revenue

### Day of Week Performance
- **Definition:** Average revenue by day of week
- **Business Use:** Optimize staffing, run day-specific promotions
- **Typical Pattern:** Higher on weekends, Friday

---

## 🎁 9. DISCOUNT & PROMOTION METRICS

### Average Discount %
- **Definition:** Average discount applied across transactions
- **Formula:** `AVG(discount_percent)`
- **Example:** 8.5%

### Discounted Sales %
- **Definition:** Percentage of transactions with a discount
- **Formula:** `(Transactions with discount / Total Transactions) × 100`
- **Example:** 35%

### Revenue from Discounts
- **Definition:** Total revenue from discounted transactions
- **Business Use:** Promotion effectiveness measurement

### Discount Impact on Margin
- **Analysis:** Compare profit margin on discounted vs non-discounted sales
- **Decision:** Determine if discounts drive enough volume to offset margin loss

---

## 📊 10. COHORT ANALYSIS METRICS

### Cohort Definition
- **Primary:** Month of first purchase (e.g., "Jan 2023 Cohort")
- **Use:** Track customer groups over time

### Cohort Size
- **Definition:** Number of customers in a cohort
- **Example:** Jan 2023 Cohort = 125 customers

### Cohort Retention %
- **Definition:** Percentage of cohort active in subsequent months
- **Formula:** `(Active customers in Month N / Cohort Size) × 100`
- **Example:**
  - Month 0: 100%
  - Month 1: 45%
  - Month 3: 32%
  - Month 6: 25%
  - Month 12: 18%

### Months Since First Purchase
- **Definition:** Time elapsed since cohort's first purchase
- **Business Use:** Track retention decay over time
- **Insight:** Identify critical drop-off points

## 💳 11. PAYMENT METHOD METRICS

### Payment Method Distribution

## 📈 13. ADVANCED CALCULATED METRICS

### Revenue CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)
- **Definition:** Smoothed annual growth rate over multiple years
- **Formula:** `((End Value / Start Value)^(1/Years) - 1) × 100`
- **Business Use:** Long-term growth assessment
- **Example:** 3-year CAGR of 22%

### Moving Average Revenue (3-Month)
- **Definition:** Average revenue over last 3 months
- **Business Use:** Smooth out volatility, identify trends
- **Formula:** `AVG(last 3 months revenue)`

### Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule)
- **Definition:** Identify customers contributing to 80% of revenue
- **Finding:** Often 20% of customers drive 80% of revenue
- **Action:** Focus retention efforts on this segment

## 🚦 KPI STATUS INDICATORS

### Traffic Light System:

**Green 🟢 - On Target**
- Revenue growth: ≥15% YoY
- Profit margin: ≥30%
- Customer retention: ≥60%

**Yellow 🟡 - Monitor**
- Revenue growth: 5-14% YoY
- Profit margin: 20-29%
- Customer retention: 40-59%

**Red 🔴 - Action Required**
- Revenue growth: <5% YoY or negative
- Profit margin: <20%
- Customer retention: <40%

## 📝 USAGE NOTES

1. **Context Matters:** Compare metrics within similar timeframes and conditions
2. **Seasonality:** Account for seasonal patterns in YoY comparisons
3. **Trends > Snapshots:** Look at metric trends, not just point-in-time values
4. **Segment Analysis:** Drill down by region, category, segment for insights
5. **Actionable Insights:** Every metric should lead to a business decision
